WhiSPa is a self-custodial, Bitcoin-only wallet built on Silent Payments
(BIP-352). You publish one reusable address, and every payment still lands at a
fresh, unrelated output on chain — so the people who pay you cannot see each
other's payments, and nobody watching the chain can tie them together.

Your keys are generated on the device from a standard recovery phrase and never
leave it. Transactions are built and signed on the phone, so no key is ever
sent to a server. The practical consequence runs both ways: nobody operating
the service can spend your bitcoin, and nobody can restore it for you either.
If you lose your recovery phrase and your device, the coins are gone.

Features

* Silent Payments (BIP-352) — one reusable address, unlinkable on chain
* A standard BIP-84 SegWit chain alongside it, for senders that need a plain
  bc1q address
* On-device transaction building and signing
* Coin control: per-coin labels, freezing, and a dust warning
* Tango — an optional two-party equal-output mix with another user, to break
  the link between your coins
* Optional app lock with biometrics or a PIN, and a separate duress PIN that
  wipes the keys
* Background chain scanning, or manual scans with a lookback you choose

What this is not

WhiSPa is not a Lightning wallet and holds no Lightning balance. It is not a
custodian, not a bank, and carries no deposit insurance of any kind.

Silent Payments remove the most common way payments get linked. They do not
make Bitcoin anonymous: chain analysis, the counterparty you transact with,
your network path and your own habits can all undo it.
